## Retrieval blocker and scientific limitation

The official Sanjesh notices and exact document paths for 1402 N2 and 1403 N1 were captured. However, direct retrieval of their linked PDF files failed through three independent routes in this run: HTTPS TLS handshake error, HTTP empty response, and stateless PDF extraction with no content. The connected Drive service then experienced a TLS authentication transport timeout when asked for archived source copies. A 1404 N2 official publication record could not be recovered before the blocker.

Accordingly, the audit deliberately made **no bank-content, key, source-type, stable-ID, migration, or progress changes**. This preserves the checksum-pinned frozen bank and avoids treating third-party-hosted files as authoritative. Any future attempt to resolve a quarantine must attach a successful official-key comparison to the exact session/question number before activation.
